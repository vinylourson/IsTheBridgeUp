The Chaban-Delmas bridge in Bordeaux lifts to let ships through, and when it
does the road is shut for an hour or two. This app tells you whether you can
cross right now, and when the next closure is.

It reads the closure schedule that Bordeaux Métropole publishes as open data,
and shows it as a verdict rather than a table: open or closed, how long until
that changes, and which ship is responsible.

FEATURES

* A plain answer first: BRIDGE OPEN or BRIDGE CLOSED, with a countdown
* The full schedule of upcoming closures, with the vessels passing
* Reminders before a closure, at as many lead times as you like, including a
  day ahead
* An optional reminder the moment the bridge reopens
* A resizable home-screen widget listing the next closures
* Works offline: the last schedule is cached and shown instantly, with the
  time it was fetched
* English and French

FORECASTS, NOT A SENSOR

There is no live feed for this bridge. The published schedule is a forecast,
set by ship movements that can shift, so treat it as a strong hint rather than
a guarantee. The app always shows when its data was last fetched so you can
judge for yourself.

PRIVACY

No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising. The app makes exactly
one kind of network request: fetching the public closure schedule from
Bordeaux Métropole's open data platform. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

Data source: Bordeaux Métropole open data, licensed under the Licence Ouverte.
